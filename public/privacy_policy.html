Summary

LingoLeaf LLC (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the LingoLeaf mobile and/or web application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

  • The App may offer Premium subscriptions via Apple in-app purchases; billing and refunds are handled by Apple.
  • Users cannot upload EPUBs; reading content is provided from a curated library.
  • We use Supabase for authentication and database/storage.
  • We may use the Google Translate API to translate user-selected text.
  • We may work with advertising partners that collect device identifiers and usage data to provide ads.

Information We Collect

1) Information you provide

  • Account information: When you create an account or sign in, we may collect your email address and basic account metadata.
  • Auth provider data: If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive information made available by that provider (typically a stable user identifier and possibly your email). We do not receive your password.
  • Reading and learning data: Such as books in your library, reading progress, highlights, saved vocabulary, notes, or translations you request within the App.
  • Support communications: If you contact us, we collect the information you include in your request.

2) Information collected automatically

  • Device & app information: Device type, operating system version, app version, language settings, and approximate location derived from IP (city/region level). We do not attempt to derive precise GPS location unless you explicitly grant it (and the App currently does not require it).
  • Usage data: Events such as screens viewed, features used, session duration, and interactions (e.g., selecting text for translation).
  • Log data: IP address, timestamps, crash reports, and performance diagnostics.
  • Identifiers: Advertising identifiers (e.g., IDFA/AAID), device identifiers, and similar identifiers used for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention (where applicable).

3) Information from third parties

  • Supabase: Provides authentication and database services that process data on our behalf.
  • Google (Translate API): When you request a translation, we may send the selected text and language parameters to Google for translation.
  • Apple / Google Sign-In: Provide authentication and may share limited profile data according to your settings with those providers.
  • Apple App Store purchases: If you subscribe through Apple in-app purchase, we may receive subscription status and transaction metadata (for example, product identifier, purchase/renewal dates, and subscription state) needed to provide Premium access. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Advertising partners: Third-party ad networks/SDKs may collect information to provide and measure ads. Your ad partners depend on your implementation (for example, Google AdMob). See “Advertising”.

How We Use Information

We use information for the following purposes:

  • Provide the App: create accounts, sign you in, sync your reading and learning progress across devices, and show your curated library.
  • Translations: process your translation requests using the Google Translate API (or similar services) and return translated text.
  • Personalization: remember preferences such as app language, learning language, and reading settings.
  • Improve performance: diagnose crashes and improve stability, speed, and UX.
  • Security & fraud prevention: protect accounts, detect abuse, and enforce our terms.
  • Advertising & measurement: deliver ads, limit repetitive ads, measure ad performance, and attribute installs/engagement where applicable.
  • Communications: respond to support requests and send important service messages (e.g., security notices).
  • Legal compliance: comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information in the conventional sense. We share information in the following situations:

Service providers (processors)

We use service providers to operate the App. These providers process information on our behalf and under our instructions. Examples include hosting, database/auth, translation processing, analytics, and customer support tooling.

Advertising partners

We may share device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, and usage signals with advertising partners to provide ads and measure performance. These partners may process information according to their own privacy policies. See “Advertising” for more details.

Legal and safety

We may disclose information if we believe it is reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect the security of the App, investigate fraud, or protect the rights, property, or safety of users or others.

Business transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

Advertising

The App is currently supported by advertising. Ads may be provided by third-party advertising partners and SDKs. These partners may collect and use information such as device identifiers (e.g., IDFA/AAID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and app usage data to:

  • deliver and display ads,
  • measure ad performance,
  • limit the number of times you see an ad (frequency capping), and
  • detect and prevent fraud or invalid traffic.

Note: If you use Google AdMob or another network, you should add the specific partner names here. If you enable personalized ads, you may need to offer opt-out mechanisms depending on your region and platform requirements.

Data Storage & Security

We store user data in our Supabase-backed infrastructure (which may include third-party cloud hosting). We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information. However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

  • Authentication: Managed through Supabase using email-based login and/or OAuth via Google and Apple.
  • Access controls: We use access controls and security rules intended to restrict user data to authorized access only.
  • Encryption: Data is typically transmitted using HTTPS/TLS. Data at rest may be encrypted by our providers depending on configuration.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the App and for legitimate business purposes, such as compliance, dispute resolution, enforcing agreements, and security. Retention periods vary depending on the type of data:

  • Account data: retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period after deletion to comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud.
  • Reading/learning data: retained while your account is active; if you delete your account, we delete or de-identify this data subject to backup and legal requirements.
  • Logs and diagnostics: retained for a limited period for security and performance analysis.

Your Choices & Rights

Account & data controls

  • Access/update: You may be able to update certain profile information within the App.
  • Delete your account: You can request account deletion clicking the "Delete Account" button in the profile page of the App.
  • Translation requests: Translation requires sending the selected text to a translation provider (e.g., Google). You can avoid this by not using the translation feature.

Advertising choices

  • Device settings: Your device may allow you to reset or limit ad tracking (e.g., iOS “Allow Apps to Request to Track”, Android Advertising ID controls).
  • Personalized ads: Depending on your region and the ad network used, you may be offered controls to opt out of personalized ads.

Regional privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@lingoleaf.app.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the age defined by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us at support@lingoleaf.app so we can take appropriate action.

International Users

If you access the App from outside the United States, your information may be processed and stored in countries where we or our service providers operate. These countries may have data protection laws different from your jurisdiction.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will provide notice within the App or by other appropriate means. The "Effective date" above indicates when this Privacy Policy was last updated.
